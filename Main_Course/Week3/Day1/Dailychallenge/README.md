# Real-world Data Analysis Scenarios
## Case Study: FC Schalke 04

### 1. Introduction

FC Schalke 04 is a professional football club in Germany. The club uses data in both its sports and business operations.

This case study explains how data analysis and data integration helped FC Schalke 04 improve its work, make better use of information, and support decisions on and off the field.

### 2. The Problem

FC Schalke 04 stored sports data and business data in different systems. This made it difficult to combine and analyse the information.

The club had a small data engineering team of three people. Much of the team's time was spent maintaining systems and preparing reports. The club needed a better way to manage its growing amount of data and support timely decisions.

### 3. The Solution

FC Schalke 04 worked with Datasolut to create a shared data platform using Microsoft Fabric and Azure Databricks.

Microsoft Fabric helped bring sports and business data together. Azure Databricks supported analytics and machine learning.

The first use case focused on combining and cleaning 220,000 membership records. This created a more reliable data foundation for understanding members and fans.

### 4. Data Analysis Methods

The case involved several data-related methods:

- Data integration: bringing data from different systems together.
- Data cleaning: combining and cleaning membership records to create more reliable information.
- Customer segmentation: using membership data to understand different groups of customers and fans.
- Analytics and machine learning: supporting business analysis and the development of a scouting platform.

### 5. Results

According to Microsoft's customer story:

- Initial data integration became 3 times faster.
- The club spent 60% less time creating reports.
- The first use case was completed in 2 months.
- 220,000 membership records were combined and cleaned.

These results helped the club reduce manual work and get more value from its data.

### 6. Impact on Business and Sports

For business operations, membership and fan data can help the club understand its audience, improve customer segmentation, and identify revenue opportunities.

For sports operations, the club is developing a scouting platform that can analyse players across multiple data sources. This can help scouts compare several players more efficiently.

### 7. What Could Have Happened Without Data Analysis?

Without data integration and improved analytics, the data team might have continued spending much of its time maintaining separate systems and preparing reports.

This could have delayed reporting and made it harder for employees to connect sports performance with business outcomes.

In my opinion, the club would have had less time and fewer resources to focus on team development and business growth.

### 8. Conclusion

The FC Schalke 04 case shows that data analysis is valuable when it helps people make better decisions.

By integrating data and reducing manual work, the club enabled its small data team to work more efficiently and supported better-informed decisions in both business and sports.
